## Eighteen historic districts and a lot of older doors

West Palm Beach has 18 locally designated historic districts and 46 individually designated sites, in neighborhoods such as Old Northwood, Northwood Hills, El Cid, Flamingo Park, Grandview Heights and Prospect/Southland Park. If your house is in one of them, sliding glass door repair in West Palm Beach comes with a question most Florida homeowners never have to ask: does this change need the city's sign-off?

For most repairs, the answer is no. The city requires a certificate of appropriateness (COA, the historic approval) only for work that changes a building's exterior appearance. New rollers inside the bottom rail don't change what anyone sees. One surprise for owners: all sides of the house are reviewed, so a rear slider counts as much as the front door once the door itself changes.

## Sliding glass door repair in West Palm Beach keeps the original look

The city leans toward repair over replacement, and so do we. The three problems we see most on any slider are worn rollers, dirty or damaged tracks and locks that won't catch. All three are fixed inside the panel and the sill, so the frame the city cares about stays exactly as it is. That's why a repair call from an older house rarely has to end in a historic review.

## What we do on an older aluminum slider

An old door gets taken apart before anyone talks about parts. We lift the panel out, lay it flat and look at the bottom rail first, because on a door this age the rail around the roller pockets is often where corrosion has eaten in. If the rail is solid, the door is a repair.

Then the old roller comes out and gets measured: wheel diameter, the housing's length, width and height, the wheel material and where the adjustment screw sits. Doors from brands that went out of business rarely have a part number we can look up, so those measurements are how we find a replacement that fits. If nothing made today matches, you'll hear that before anything is ordered.

The track gets the same attention. Decades of a steel wheel riding on aluminum can wear a groove into the top of the rail. A worn rail can often take a cap, a thin cover that gives the new rollers a smooth surface again, instead of a new frame. Last comes the height adjustment, and then the latch, because a panel that sat low for years will have pulled the lock out of line with its keeper. Our page on [sliding door roller replacement](/sliding-door-roller-replacement) covers the roller side in more detail.

## When an old door has to go

A new door needs a building permit, a Florida product approval rated for the wind loads at your house, and installation that follows the approval documents. Palm Beach County is outside the High-Velocity Hurricane Zone, so you don't need the HVHZ approval required in [Miami](/sliding-glass-door-repair-miami), but the county sits in the wind-borne debris region, where glass doors need impact glass or approved protection. In a historic district, get the COA first, because the style it approves decides which product goes on the permit. Our page on [sliding glass door replacement](/sliding-glass-door-replacement) explains approvals and impact glass.

## Before you call about an older door

Check the city's historic property map to see whether your house is designated. Then send us a photo of the whole door from outside and both bottom corners of the moving panel. If the screen drags too, mention it, since its small rollers can be swapped on the same visit. A hinged French door is a different job, and we'll tell you what its hinges and strike need.
