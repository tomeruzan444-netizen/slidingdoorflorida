## Eighteen historic districts and a lot of older doors

West Palm Beach has 18 locally designated historic districts and 46 individually designated sites, in neighborhoods such as Old Northwood, Northwood Hills, El Cid, Flamingo Park, Grandview Heights and Prospect/Southland Park. If your house is in one of them, sliding glass door repair in West Palm Beach comes with a question most Florida homeowners never have to ask: does this change need the city's sign-off?

For most repairs, the answer is no. The city requires a certificate of appropriateness (COA, the historic approval) for work that changes a building's exterior appearance, and it doesn't review interior remodeling. New rollers inside the bottom rail don't change what anyone sees from the street.

## The back of the house counts too

Many people assume a slider on the back porch is out of sight and out of review. The city doesn't see it that way. All sides of the building are reviewed, for both contributing and noncontributing properties.

So a door that opens from the kitchen onto a rear patio is just as much a part of the review as the front door. The difference is in who does the reviewing, and that depends on which wall the door is on. Anyone typing patio door repair near me about a rear slider can relax a little, though: the review only matters once the door itself changes.

## Sliding glass door repair in West Palm Beach keeps the original look

The city leans toward repair over replacement. It recommends repairing original wood windows where possible, says vinyl generally isn't permitted for windows, and points to aluminum impact products when a window has to be replaced in kind. It doesn't spell out sliding doors the same way, but the direction is clear.

That suits the way we work. The three problems we see most on any slider are worn rollers, dirty or damaged tracks and locks that won't catch. A sliding glass door roller replacement and a cleaned track fix most doors that drag, and neither touches the frame you'd need approval to change. A sliding door handle replacement that matches the old style, or a patio door lock replacement inside the panel, stays out of sight too. It's also why a search for sliding door repair near me from an older house rarely has to end in a historic review.

Parts are the hard part on older doors. Aluminum sliders from brands that went out of business, and older impact models, can take real digging to find sliding door rollers and locks for. We match the replacement by measurement, and if the right one isn't made anymore, we'll say so before anyone orders anything.

## Permits and wind loads outside the HVHZ

A new door needs a building permit. A permit is required for replacing windows and doors, the products must meet the wind loads in West Palm Beach, and they must be installed according to the manufacturer's product approval documents.

Palm Beach County is outside the High-Velocity Hurricane Zone, so a door here doesn't need the HVHZ approval required in [Miami](/sliding-glass-door-repair-miami). It still needs a Florida product approval rated for the pressures at your house. All of unincorporated Palm Beach County is in the wind-borne debris region, where glazed openings need impact glass or approved protection. Our page on [sliding glass door replacement](/sliding-glass-door-replacement) explains product approvals and the impact question in more detail.

In a historic district, a replacement means two approvals, the COA and the building permit. Get the COA first, because the style it approves decides which product goes on the permit.
