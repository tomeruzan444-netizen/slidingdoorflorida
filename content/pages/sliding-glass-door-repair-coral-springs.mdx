## Built to a plan, and still run like one

In Coral Springs you can paint your front door any color you like. The rest of the house is another matter. City rules keep blue, red, orange, purple, black and colors that are too dark or bright off the main walls.

Here's what that means for sliding glass door repair in Coral Springs. A repair leaves the outside of the house as it was. A replacement is where rules and paperwork start.

That attention to looks goes back to the start. The city was incorporated in July 1963, and its developer, Coral Ridge Properties, set out to build one of the first truly planned cities in Florida.

## What a repair changes, and what it doesn't

New rollers, a cleaned track, an adjusted latch or a new handle all happen inside the existing frame. Nobody walking past can tell, and no new product goes into the wall. That's a good reason to fix a door before replacing it, as long as the frame is sound.

A replacement changes the frame color, the panel layout and the glass. If your community has a homeowners association, its architectural rules may be stricter than the city's, so get its answer in writing before you order.

## Kids, pools and sliding glass door repair in Coral Springs

A slider in a house with children gets opened and shut all day in summer, slammed, leaned on and run into. Three things deserve attention.

The glass first. Federal rules list patio-type sliding glass doors among the openings that need safety glazing, which in practice means tempered or laminated glass. Treat a crack as a safety problem, not a cosmetic one.

Then the pool. A new residential pool in Florida needs at least one safety feature to pass final inspection under the state's pool safety law. One option is an exit alarm on every door and window with direct access to the pool.

Another option is a self-closing, self-latching device on those doors, with its release at least 54 inches above the floor. If the alarm or latch on your pool-side slider has stopped working, fix it along with the door.

And the screen. Kids and pets push through screens, and a screen off its track keeps nothing out. Sliding screen door repair is usually new mesh and spline, sometimes a tougher pet-resistant mesh, plus the small spring rollers that hold the panel on its rail.

## The everyday wear

Three parts account for most of what we fix, rollers, tracks and locks, so that's where a sliding door repair visit starts. Worn sliding door rollers make a door heavy, which is when kids start yanking it, and a sliding glass door roller replacement with a cleaned track often fixes that.

A door that won't lock has often dropped on a worn roller, so the latch misses its keeper. Raising the panel can solve it before any patio door lock replacement. A snapped pull is a sliding door handle replacement, matched by screw spacing, not by eye.

If the door won't budge, don't force it. A panel pulled hard off its track can bend the rail.
