## A 1992 door in 2026

If your Pembroke Pines house still has the slider it was built with, that door is probably more than 30 years old. The median home in the city was built in 1992, and 37.6% of all homes went up in the 1990s alone. That age shapes most sliding glass door repair in Pembroke Pines.

The frame and the glass often have plenty of life left after 30 years. The small wheels underneath, the latch and the weatherstrip usually don't.

## Older parts and sliding glass door repair in Pembroke Pines

Worn rollers, dirty or damaged tracks and misaligned locks are the three problems our founder sees most often, and a 1990s slider tends to have at least one. Rollers wear flat or seize, the panel drops a little, and then it drags and the latch misses its keeper.

On a door this age, finding the right part is the harder half. Older impact models, and aluminum doors from brands that stopped making parts, can take real digging to match.

## How we match parts on a 30-year-old slider

Nothing gets ordered from a photo. The old roller comes out of the panel first, and we measure what actually fits: wheel diameter, the housing's length, width and height, whether the wheel is steel or plastic, and where the adjustment screw sits. Two rollers can look identical in a listing and still be a few millimeters off, and a few millimeters is the difference between a door that glides and one that rubs.

Handles and locks get the same treatment. For a handle, it's the distance between the mounting screws, measured center to center, and the thickness of the panel the screws pass through. For the lock inside the panel, it's the size of the lock body and where its screws line up. A handle that matches the look but not the holes is no use.

When a part really isn't made anymore, we'll say so before you've paid for anything. Sometimes a close match works with a small adjustment. Sometimes it doesn't, and that's part of the repair-or-replace conversation.

## The screen panel in front

The screen rides on smaller rollers in a lighter frame and jumps its track more easily than the glass panel. If yours has fallen out, lift it back into its top channel first. If it falls out again, its rollers are probably worn. Our [sliding screen door repair](/sliding-screen-door-repair) page covers the rollers, the mesh and the spline that holds it in.

## Before a new door goes in

We'd rather repair than sell a new door when the frame is solid. Severe frame damage, compromised glass, or corrosion spread through several parts at once is where Joseph says replacement becomes the better investment.

A replacement is permit work. The city asks for the county's uniform window and door schedule, product approvals and design wind pressures, plus a Homeowners Association Affidavit of Awareness even if you don't belong to an association. Broward is in the High-Velocity Hurricane Zone, so the new door needs an approval for that zone, and the code treats homes built before September 1, 1994 differently on impact protection. With a 1992 median, Pembroke Pines has plenty of houses on each side of that date.

Photos of both bottom corners of the panel and the full length of the track tell us most of what we need to know.
