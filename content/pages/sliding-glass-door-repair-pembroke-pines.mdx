## Older parts and sliding glass door repair in Pembroke Pines

Worn rollers, dirty or damaged tracks and misaligned locks are the three problems our founder sees most often in sliding door repair, and a 1990s slider tends to have at least one. Sliding door rollers wear flat or seize, the panel drops a little, and then it drags and the latch misses its keeper.

On a door this age, finding the right part is the harder half. Older impact models, and aluminum doors from brands that stopped making parts, can take real digging to match. Two rollers can look the same in a listing and still differ, so we measure the old one first. Sliding glass door roller replacement on a door like that is a matching job first.

Hardware works the same way. A sliding door handle replacement or a patio door lock replacement on a 30-year-old door starts with the screw spacing and the lock body. A handle that matches the look but not the holes is no use.

A search for sliding glass door repair near me turns up plenty of names. For a 1990s door, the useful question to ask any of them is whether they can identify your model and find parts for it.

## Screen color is an association question

If your slider has a screen panel in front of it, that panel wears on its own schedule. It rides on smaller rollers in a lighter frame and jumps its track more easily. Our [sliding screen door repair](/sliding-screen-door-repair) page covers the rollers, the mesh and the spline that holds it in.

<PagePhoto name="screen-height" />

Plenty of people search sliding door repair near me when the glass door is fine and the screen has jumped its track. Lift the screen back into its top channel first. If it falls out again, its rollers are probably worn.

Before you change how the screen looks, check with your association. In an HOA community, read the association's rules, covenants and deed restrictions and get HOA approval before a project starts, because skipping it can end in costly civil disputes. A darker mesh, a solar screen or a new frame color is the kind of change to ask about. Rescreening with the same mesh changes nothing a neighbor would notice.
