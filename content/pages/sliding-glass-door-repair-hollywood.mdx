## Start at the sill

On a Hollywood slider, the first thing we look at is the bottom track. The median home in the city was built in 1969, and about three out of four homes went up before 1980. Decades of sand, salt and rain wear an aluminum sill down, so most sliding glass door repair in Hollywood starts there, before anyone touches the rollers.

The sill does two jobs. It's the rail the rollers ride on, and it's a channel that catches rain and drains it out through small slots on the outside face called weep holes. When those clog, water that should leave the track rises over the inside lip and onto your floor.

A call during a rainstorm usually means exactly that problem. Before you call, brush out the weep holes and pour a little water into the track on a dry day. If it sits instead of draining, the holes are still blocked.

## Wind-driven rain and an older track

Storm leaks get blamed on the glass, but the track is a common way in. After Hurricane Ian, inspectors found wind-driven rain got into homes mainly through sliding glass doors and soffits.

An older rail gives that water an easier path. Pitting, a bent lip or a worn ridge where the rollers run all let water and grit collect. Our page on [sliding door track repair](/sliding-door-track-repair) explains when a cap over the old rail is enough and when the track has to be replaced. That's why sliding door repair on an older Hollywood slider starts down at the track.

## Salt from the beach side

Hollywood faces the ocean, with a 2.2-mile paved Broadwalk running beside the beach. Our founder's view, from years of these jobs, is that coastal homes need more frequent maintenance than homes inland: salt speeds up corrosion on metal parts, and humidity and sand add their own wear.

Near the water, that shows up as pitted tracks and seized sliding door rollers. A sliding door roller replacement on a pitted rail won't last, which is why the track comes first. Salt settles in the sill and stays damp there for days after rain. Vacuum the track, wipe it with fresh water, and keep heavy grease out of it, since grease holds sand.

## The rest of a sliding glass door repair in Hollywood

Once the track is sound, the rest of the door is usually parts. Sliding glass door roller replacement brings a dragging panel back up to height. A latch that stopped catching often just needs adjusting once the panel sits right, and a patio door lock replacement is for a lock body that's worn out. On older doors, sliding door handle replacement means matching the screw spacing to the holes already in the frame.

The screen panel has its own small rollers and gets the same salt. Sliding screen door repair is quicker than work on the glass panel, and it's worth doing at the same visit.

Whoever you call, ask whether they check the sill before the rollers, and ask before anyone orders parts.

## Repair or replace an older Hollywood door

We'd rather do a sliding patio door repair than sell you a new door when the frame is solid. Sliding glass door replacement makes sense when the frame is corroded through, the glass is compromised, or several parts are failing at once.

Broward County is in the Florida Building Code's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone, along with Miami-Dade. A replacement door here is permit work, and the product needs an approval that covers that zone. In a historic district, talk to the city's planning staff before you order.

From a house a few blocks off the beach, you can save a step: send photos of the track, both bottom corners of the panel, and any label on the glass.
