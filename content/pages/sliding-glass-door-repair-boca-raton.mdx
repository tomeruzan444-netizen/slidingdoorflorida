## The hurricane rules change at the county line

Boca Raton is in Palm Beach County, outside the Florida Building Code's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone, which covers only Broward and Miami-Dade. A few minutes south in Deerfield Beach, a new door is held to HVHZ product approval. In Boca Raton it isn't. For the sliding glass door repair Boca Raton homeowners usually need, that line rarely matters. It matters the day a door has to be replaced.

Outside the HVHZ doesn't mean outside the storm rules. In Palm Beach County, the wind-borne debris region covers areas within a mile of the coast where the design wind speed is 130 mph or more, plus anywhere it reaches 140 mph, and all of unincorporated Palm Beach County is inside that region. Expect the same in Boca Raton, and let the city's building division confirm it for your address.

Inside that region, the Florida Building Code calls for glazed openings to be impact resistant or covered by approved impact protection. So a sliding glass door replacement here usually means impact glass or approved shutters, with a Florida product approval for use outside the HVHZ.

## Sliding glass door repair in Boca Raton on multi-panel doors

A three- or four-panel slider, a stacking door or a pocketing glass wall works like a two-panel patio door with more of everything. Each moving panel rides on its own sliding door rollers, with its own height adjustment.

That's why one panel can drag while the rest glide. On a multi-slide door, the panel that gets opened most tends to wear first, and a sliding door roller replacement on that panel alone is often the whole job. The method: take the panel's weight off with a block of wood, uncover the adjustment hole, and turn with a 5 mm hex wrench until the gap against the jamb is even. So if one panel is the stubborn one, the fix may be that panel's rollers, not the whole glass wall.

The sill matters as much as the rollers. Multi-slide sills drain through weep systems, with a sill pan under them in every installation. A pocket door adds a pan inside the wall pocket, with a 4-inch end dam on every side of it.

Where the panels meet, interlocks and the keeper have to line up. When one panel sags, the lock stops catching, so we adjust the height before talking about a patio door lock replacement. Our page on [patio door repair](/patio-door-repair) covers the other door types, from French doors to stacking systems.

The city has an over-the-counter permit for windows, doors and impact shutters on single-family homes. Condos and commercial buildings use a separate checklist. The single-family path asks for these, in order.

## Repairs that keep the approval you already have

New rollers, a straightened track or a new lock don't swap the door for a different product. Most sliding door repair stays at that level: sliding glass door roller replacement, a cleaned and adjusted track, or a sliding door handle replacement when the old one has snapped or worked loose. Sliding screen door repair fits into the same visit. If you expected to hear the door has to go, this level of repair is the more likely answer.

We'd rather fix a sound frame than sell you a new door. Replacement makes sense when the frame is badly damaged, the glass is compromised, or corrosion has spread through several parts. Parts are the other limit. Older impact doors, and aluminum doors from brands that are gone, can take extra work to source rollers and locks for, and we'll tell you early if we can't find the right one.

## Appearance review before anything changes outside

Boca Raton's Community Appearance Board reviews aesthetic standards for site development and building alterations within the city, and planning applications need its formal recommendation. Your homeowners association may run its own architectural review as well.

A repair doesn't change how the house looks. A new frame color, a wider opening or a pocketing door where a slider used to be does, so ask the association and the city's planning staff before you order.
