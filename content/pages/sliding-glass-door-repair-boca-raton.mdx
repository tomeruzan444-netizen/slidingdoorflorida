## Big glass walls, one panel at a time

A lot of the doors we see in Boca Raton aren't simple two-panel sliders. They're three- and four-panel sliders, stacking doors and pocketing glass walls that open a whole room to the pool. Sliding glass door repair in Boca Raton on a door like that works the same way as on any slider, with more of everything. Each moving panel rides on its own rollers, with its own height adjustment.

That's why one panel can drag while the rest glide. The panel that gets opened most tends to wear first, and new rollers on that panel alone are often the whole job. So if one panel is the stubborn one, the fix may be that panel's rollers, not the whole glass wall.

## How we work through a multi-panel door

We start by running every panel on its own, end to end, and noting which ones drag, rattle or stop short. Then we look at the gaps. Each panel should sit with an even gap against the jamb and the panel next to it, and a gap that narrows at the bottom tells us which corner has dropped.

Adjusting a panel means taking its weight off first. We lift it slightly with a block of wood, uncover the adjustment hole, and turn with a 5 mm hex wrench until the gap is even. Lifting with the wrench alone strains the roller and can strip the adjustment. If a roller is worn past adjusting, the panel comes out and the roller is measured and replaced.

Where the panels meet, the interlocks (the overlapping edges that seal one panel to the next) and the lock keeper have to line up. When one panel sags, the lock stops catching, so we set the heights before talking about a new lock.

The sill matters as much as the rollers. Multi-slide sills drain through weep systems, and a pocketing door adds a hidden problem, because whatever blows into the wall pocket stays there. Leaves, sand and pool debris build up at the bottom of the pocket, and the panel ends up grinding over them every time it slides in. We clean the pocket while the panel is out. Our page on [patio door repair](/patio-door-repair) covers the other door types, from French doors to stacking systems.

## Repairs that keep the approval you already have

New rollers, a straightened track or a new lock don't swap the door for a different product, so they don't touch the door's product approval or need a permit. Most repairs stay at that level, including the screen panel's rollers on the same visit.

We'd rather fix a sound frame than sell you a new door. Replacement makes sense when the frame is badly damaged, the glass is compromised, or corrosion has spread through several parts. Parts are the other limit. Older impact doors, and aluminum doors from brands that are gone, can take extra work to source rollers and locks for, and we'll tell you early if we can't find the right one.

## When a door is replaced in Boca Raton

Boca Raton is in Palm Beach County, outside the High-Velocity Hurricane Zone that covers Broward and Miami-Dade. A few minutes south in Deerfield Beach, a new door is held to HVHZ product approval. In Boca Raton it isn't. The county is still in the wind-borne debris region, though, so a new glass door needs impact glass or approved shutters, with a Florida product approval for use outside the HVHZ.

The city has an over-the-counter permit for windows, doors and impact shutters on single-family homes. Condos and commercial buildings use a separate checklist.

If a door won't move, send us a photo of the whole door from inside, a close-up of the bottom corner of the panel that drags, and the sill, and tell us how many panels move. That's usually enough to tell a roller job from a bigger one.
