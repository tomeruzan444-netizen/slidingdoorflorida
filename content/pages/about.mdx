## What years on sliding doors have taught us

Most door problems stop being mysteries once you've seen enough of them. The three we run into most are worn-out rollers, dirty or damaged tracks, and locks that have worn out or slipped out of line. They're usually connected, which is why our [sliding glass door repair guide](/) starts from what the door is doing rather than which part to buy.

The coast is harder on doors than anywhere inland. Salt speeds up corrosion on every metal part, and humidity and sand wear the rest, so a waterfront home needs more maintenance than a house a few miles in. We see that difference on our jobs all the time.

Old doors are their own puzzle. On older impact models and on aluminum doors from brands that are gone, finding the right part can take real digging, and the hard part is usually identifying the exact model. More on that on our [patio door repair](/patio-door-repair) page.

And the most expensive mistakes tend to happen before anyone calls us: forcing a heavy door, adjusting rollers without knowing how the door is put together, or ordering a roller because it looks right. Our [roller replacement](/sliding-door-roller-replacement) page explains how to measure one properly.

## An honest repair-or-replace call

We won't recommend a repair just to get a job, and we won't sell you a door you don't need. When the frame is badly damaged, the glass is compromised, or corrosion has spread through several parts at once, a new door is the better long-term value, and that's what you'll hear from us. When it's worn rollers and a dirty track, you'll hear that instead, even if you called expecting to buy a door. Our [replacement page](/sliding-glass-door-replacement) lays out where that line sits.

## How our crews work

Every crew works the same way, wherever the job is. We check the track before we touch the rollers, because new rollers on a damaged rail wear out fast. We match parts by measurement, not by how they look. And you hear what we found and what we'd do about it before any work starts.
