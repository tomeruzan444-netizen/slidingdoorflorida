## What we work on

Each of these has its own page with the causes, the checks you can do yourself, and what the repair involves:

- [Roller replacement](/sliding-door-roller-replacement) for doors that drag, grind or take two hands
- [Track repair](/sliding-door-track-repair) for bent, pitted or flooding tracks and sills
- [Lock and handle repair](/sliding-door-lock-and-handle-repair) for latches that won't catch and broken handles
- [Screen door repair](/sliding-screen-door-repair) for torn mesh, bent frames and screens that fall out
- [Patio door repair](/patio-door-repair) for sliders, French doors and multi-slide systems
- [Closet door repair](/sliding-closet-door-repair) for bypass and mirrored doors that jump the track
- [Door replacement](/sliding-glass-door-replacement) when the frame itself is finished

If you're not sure which one you're dealing with, the [sliding glass door repair](/) guide starts from what the door is doing.
