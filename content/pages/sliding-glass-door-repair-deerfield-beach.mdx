## Two kinds of condo slider in one city

For a condo owner, sliding glass door repair in Deerfield Beach depends a lot on where the building is. Some condos are close to the ocean, with a slider that opens onto a balcony and takes salt air every day. Others are inland in Century Village East, where separate condominium buildings, each with its own name, sit inside one large community off Hillsboro Boulevard.

The parts that fail are the same in both: rollers, tracks and locks, the three problems our founder sees most in Florida homes. What changes is how fast they wear, and who has to sign off before anything about the door changes.

## When sliding glass door repair in Deerfield Beach means a lighter door

A slider that takes your whole body weight to open isn't something you have to live with. The panel sits on two small wheels in its bottom rail. When the sliding door rollers wear flat or their bearings seize, the wheels skid instead of turning, and the door gets harder to move every month. If that's what sent you searching for sliding glass door repair near me, the cause is almost always at the bottom of the panel.

The usual fix is a sliding glass door roller replacement plus a cleaned track. We check the track first, because new wheels on a pitted rail wear out early. Then we match the rollers by measurement, not looks, and set the panel just high enough to clear the sill without letting air and water past the seals.

The lock tends to come along for the ride. When a roller wears, that end of the panel drops and the latch lands below its keeper, so raising the panel often fixes the latch too. A snapped handle needs a sliding door handle replacement, and a bent or worn latch hook needs a patio door lock replacement. Neither one means a new door.

## What the association and Broward County each decide

Sliding patio door repair that swaps worn parts leaves the door as it was. A new door is a different matter, and in Deerfield Beach up to three sets of rules can apply.

The building code comes first. Broward County is in the Florida Building Code's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone, so a replacement door needs a product approval that covers HVHZ use. Broward also has window and door replacement permits filed on one shared county form, which lists each opening with its product approval, design pressure and glass type.

Then there's the association. Under Florida condo law, a condo board can't turn down hurricane protection that meets the specifications it adopted, but it can hold you to the building's existing outside appearance. So a sliding glass door replacement in either kind of building should look like your neighbors' doors from the outside.

The city can have a say too. Deerfield Beach's Community Appearance Board reviews the exterior look of multi-family properties, and in 2008 it took up a paint change for one of the Century Village East condominiums. Ask the city's planning staff whether a door change needs it.

## Screens get opened more than the glass

On a ground-floor unit or a balcony, the screen is often the panel that moves most, because it stays shut while the breeze comes through. It rides on its own small rollers, and when they wear, the screen drags or jumps off the track. If a screen that won't stay on its rail had you typing patio door repair near me, it's usually the smallest fix of the visit.
