## A city planned so no two houses match

Plantation was laid out so neighbors wouldn't live in copies of each other's homes. Frederick Peters hired architect Russell Pancoast to draw the master plan, and it included an ordinance that kept homes next to each other from looking alike.

That's what makes sliding glass door repair in Plantation a door-by-door job. Two houses a few lots apart can have doors of different widths, makers and decades, so the door next door tells you very little about yours.

## Why the neighbor's roller may not fit your door

There's no universal roller. We match parts by measurement, not looks, so every sliding glass door roller replacement starts with a tape measure: wheel diameter, housing size, and how the roller mounts in the bottom rail of the panel. A part that looks right in a photo can sit a fraction too low and drag.

Older doors are the hard ones. The city was incorporated in April 1953, and some aluminum sliders here come from brands that stopped making parts long ago. Older impact models are in the same group. Identifying the exact model can take longer than the swap itself.

If your door has been repaired before, don't assume the parts in it are original.

## Shade trees and the sill track

Plantation takes its trees seriously. The city has been named a Tree City USA and offers residents a free shade tree for the swale. Shade helps a door, since plastic rollers and guides break down faster in direct sun.

The trade-off shows up at the bottom. Leaves, seed pods and grit settle into the sill track and the weep holes, the small drain slots on the outside face of the sill. When those clog, water sits in the track after rain and the sliding door rollers grind through whatever is down there.

Vacuum the track and brush out the weep holes first. It's also the first thing we check, since a dirty or dented rail wears out new rollers fast. If the door suddenly turned heavy, that check is worth five minutes.

## Sliding glass door repair in Plantation, or a new door

Rollers, tracks and locks are the three most common problems we see in Florida homes, and most sliding door repair comes down to one of them. A door that feels impossible to move is often a sliding door roller replacement plus a cleaned, adjusted track. A worn lock is a patio door lock replacement, and a cracked pull is a sliding door handle replacement, as long as a matching part exists.

The frame decides the rest. If it's straight, the bottom rail is solid and the glass is intact, we'd rather fix the door than sell you a new one. If you've been told the door is finished, ask about the frame first.

When the frame is badly damaged, the glass is compromised, or corrosion has spread across several failing parts, sliding glass door replacement is the better use of your money, and we'll say so.

## If the door has to go

A new door is a permit job, filed by a contractor registered with the city or by an owner-builder who meets the plan reviewer first, and the city permit doesn't stand in for your HOA's approval. Broward is in the High-Velocity Hurricane Zone, so the age of your house can change what a new door has to meet.
