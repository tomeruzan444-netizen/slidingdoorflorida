## Start with how old the door is

Before anyone orders a part, find out roughly how old your door is. For sliding glass door repair in Hialeah, that one fact decides more than the symptom does. Half of the city's homes were built in 1975 or earlier, and more than three quarters before 1990.

If your house is one of those and the slider has never been replaced, the rollers and lock inside it may come from a maker that no longer exists. That's often the first thing you learn after calling around for a part nobody stocks.

That doesn't make the door a lost cause. Most sliding patio door repair on an old door comes down to the same three parts as on a new one: rollers, track and lock. The real question is whether those parts can still be found.

## Old door triage for sliding glass door repair in Hialeah

On an older slider, we work through the door in a set order. You can do the first three yourself, which also helps you describe the door properly when you phone.

1. **Look for a name.** Makers often etch their name into a corner of the glass or put a label on the edge of the frame. A brand and model turn a parts hunt into a lookup.
2. **Measure, don't match by eye.** Two rollers can look the same and still differ in the housing or the wheel. Our founder sees parts bought on looks alone as one of the most common mistakes homeowners make.
3. **Check the bottom rail of the panel.** If the aluminum there is split or eaten through, there's nothing solid left to hold a roller.
4. **Then the hunt.** On older impact models, and on aluminum doors from brands that are long gone, finding the right roller, handle or lock can take real digging. For a sliding door handle replacement on a discontinued door, the screw spacing and the panel thickness matter more than the brand name on the old handle.

Sometimes the part turns up. Sometimes the honest answer is that the door has outlived its parts, and we'll say so rather than force a near fit. A patio door lock replacement on a door like this can mean matching an old lock body by its faceplate and screw holes rather than by name.

If your slider is still a current model, the [roller replacement](/sliding-door-roller-replacement) page walks through measuring, adjusting and choosing a wheel.

## Heat and sun do more here than salt

Hialeah sits inland, away from the bay and the beaches. Our founder's experience is that homes farther from the ocean need less frequent maintenance than coastal ones, because salt is what speeds up corrosion on the metal parts. That changes what sliding door repair here usually involves.

That leaves heat and UV. The plastic wheels on sliding door rollers, and plastic guides and handle pieces, break down in sun over the years. A slider facing west takes the full afternoon, so sliding glass door roller replacement can come around sooner on that side of the house.

When a plastic wheel cracks or flattens, sliding door roller replacement is the fix, and inland a good nylon or steel roller is usually fine. Stainless costs more and mostly earns it near salt water. The small plastic wheels on the screen panel bake too, so sliding screen door repair can come up on the same visit.

A phone call about a 1960s house with a dead roller only goes so far. A photo of the bottom corner of the panel and the label on the glass gets you an actual answer about parts.

## If the door is past saving

Rollers or a lock normally don't need a permit. A new door does, from Hialeah's own building department, and because Miami-Dade is in the High-Velocity Hurricane Zone, it needs an approval for HVHZ use, usually a Miami-Dade NOA (Notice of Acceptance).

Older houses get one narrow break. Under the Florida Building Code, a one- or two-family home in the HVHZ built under codes in force before September 1, 1994 can replace up to 25% of its total glass area within 12 months without adding impact protection. The new products still have to be designed for the code's wind pressures.

Look back at the table, and you'll see that cutoff covers most of the city. Your permit office decides whether it applies to your house, so ask before you price a new door.

Northeast of Hialeah, [Miami Gardens](/sliding-glass-door-repair-miami-gardens) has houses of a similar age with a different weak spot, the latch. To the southeast, most homes in [Miami](/sliding-glass-door-repair-miami) are in large buildings, where salt and wind come first.
