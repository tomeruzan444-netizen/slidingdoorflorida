## Two towns, two kinds of door trouble

Pompano Beach is two towns joined into one. The farming town of Pompano was incorporated in 1908, and in 1947 it merged with the newer municipality on the beach to become the City of Pompano Beach.

The split still matters for sliding glass door repair in Pompano Beach. A balcony slider facing the Atlantic and a patio door on a mainland street wear out in different ways.

The city has about three miles of beach. The barrier island is mostly residential, with a broader mix of housing types than the mainland.

## Salt on the barrier island

On an oceanfront balcony, salt is the main enemy. Wind carries it onto the door, and it settles in the sill track and stays damp after rain.

Many sliding door rollers put steel bearings inside an aluminum panel, riding on an aluminum track. When two different metals touch with salt water between them, one corrodes in place of the other, and aluminum is the metal that gives way.

Homes near the ocean need more frequent maintenance than homes inland. A sliding door roller replacement on a beachfront unit is a good moment to ask whether stainless steel rollers exist in your door's size. They cost more and hold up better near salt water, but only if the housing fits your panel exactly.

A sliding patio door repair on a high balcony has one more complication. Pulling a heavy panel out of its frame several floors up is a two-person job, and wind makes it harder.

## Canal and Intracoastal homes

The beach isn't the only water. The Intracoastal, the Cypress Creek Canal, the Hillsboro Inlet and the finger canals and isles serve 2,487 single-family waterfront properties. Those sliders sit farther from the surf but still face damp air and the sand that comes back from the boat or the pool.

Sand mixed with old grease turns into a paste that grinds roller wheels down and scores the track. Vacuum the rail and clear the weep holes, the drain slots on the outside face of the sill, before the door gets heavy.

## Screens that hold up near salt water

Aluminum screen isn't a good fit for coastal or humid climates. Fiberglass, sun control screens and the tougher pet-resistant meshes hold up better. Fiberglass is coated glass yarn, so it won't rust.

Screen panels ride on small spring rollers that corrode too. Our page on [sliding screen door repair](/sliding-screen-door-repair) covers mesh choices, spline sizes and why a screen keeps falling off its track. If you searched sliding door repair near me because the screen came off in the wind, start there.

## Sliding glass door repair in Pompano Beach, and when a permit comes in

In Pompano Beach, installing, replacing or doing a major repair on any window or door requires a building permit. A permit isn't required to replace a part that doesn't alter the door's approval or make it unsafe.

Most sliding door repair never reaches that line. New rollers, a patio door lock replacement or a sliding door handle replacement are parts on the door you already have.

Replacement is different. Installing or replacing exterior doors counts as structural work, so it never qualifies for the new exemption for small projects under $7,500. Owner-builder permits don't apply to work in condominiums.

We'll tell you which side of that line your door is on. Frame corrosion that has spread, compromised glass or several failing parts at once point toward a sliding glass door replacement. A sliding glass door roller replacement and a cleaned track point the other way.
