## Horse country, not beach country

Horses have been part of life in Davie for generations, and the town has a trail system of more than 187 miles. It's also inland, west of Fort Lauderdale. So sliding glass door repair in Davie deals with a different problem than the coast does. Here it's grit, not salt.

Our founder says homes near the ocean need maintenance more often than homes further inland. That holds in Davie. A slider here rarely has the salt-pitted track of a beach condo, but dirt, sand and debris still find their way into the sill, and every grain adds friction under the rollers.

## Sliding glass door repair in Davie starts with grit in the track

Dust from yards, trails and pool decks settles in the bottom track and mixes with any grease left there. The paste it makes grinds the roller wheels down and scores the rail. A door that used to glide starts taking two hands.

Vacuum the track end to end before you do anything else, then check the small weep holes on the outside face of the sill. If the door still drags with a clean track, the sliding door rollers are worn or have settled low. On every sliding door repair, we check the track before the rollers on every door, because new wheels on a scored rail wear out early.

Inland, a good nylon or steel roller usually does the job. Sliding glass door roller replacement here is mostly about matching the size and the weight rating.

## About one Davie home in seven is a mobile home

Mobile homes make up 14.0% of Davie's housing units.

Sliding glass doors built for manufactured homes fall under federal rules. They must be certified to a recognized manufactured-housing door standard and carry a certification label. That label is a good place to start identifying the door. Parts are matched by measurement either way, since a roller from a site-built slider can look right and still not fit.

## Pool doors and the latch

Florida's pool safety law puts a slider that opens onto the pool in a special spot. The law requires a new residential pool to have at least one of five safety features before it passes final inspection. Two involve doors: an exit alarm rated at least 85 dB A at 10 feet, or a self-closing, self-latching device with its release at least 54 inches off the floor.

Both depend on a door that closes all the way. A panel that has dropped on worn rollers stops short, the latch misses, and an alarm sensor may not read the door as shut. Whether the law applies to your existing home is a question for the town's building division, not us.

A new lock body fixes a lock that's worn out inside, and a new handle often goes along with it, since the two mount through the same holes. Our page on [lock and handle repair](/sliding-door-lock-and-handle-repair) covers measuring, mortise locks and the security layers on a slider.

If the pool door won't latch, start with the panel height. The lock is often fine.

## When a Davie door is worth replacing

We'd rather do a sliding patio door repair than sell a new door when the frame is solid. A sliding glass door replacement makes sense when the frame is badly damaged, the glass is compromised, or several parts are failing at once.

Davie is in Broward County, one of the two counties in the Florida Building Code's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone. A new door needs a permit and a product approval that covers that zone, even this far from the beach. Our [Plantation](/sliding-glass-door-repair-plantation) page, just north of Davie, explains how the year a house was built can change what a replacement has to meet.
