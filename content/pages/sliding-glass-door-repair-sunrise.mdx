## Condo doors in the original Sunrise neighborhoods

Sunrise began in 1961 as Sunrise Golf Village and dropped "Golf Village" from its name after a 1971 referendum. The original east side is single-family streets mixed with apartments and condominiums near the former golf course.

In a condo, sliding glass door repair in Sunrise starts with a question a house never raises: who is responsible for the door. The answer is in your building's paperwork, and it's worth finding before anyone orders parts.

## When a door takes two hands to open

A sliding glass door in good shape should roll with a steady push from one hand. If you have to brace your feet, pull with both arms or lift the handle to get it moving, the door is telling you something.

On a sliding door repair call like this, the cause usually sits at the bottom. Worn sliding door rollers flatten or seize, the panel drops onto the track, and every inch becomes friction. Dirt, sand and old grease in the rail make it worse.

A sliding door roller replacement and a cleaned track can turn a door that fights you into one that moves with a light push. We check the track before the rollers, because new wheels on a damaged rail don't last. And don't force it: yanking a stuck door can crack a roller housing, bend the track or pull a handle loose.

## Handles and locks that need a firm grip

A lock that only catches when you lean on the door usually isn't broken. When the panel sags, the latch hook drops below the keeper. Raising the panel can fix the lock without touching it, which is why a patio door lock replacement is the second thing we look at, not the first.

Handles are a separate wear point. A cracked or wobbly pull is a sliding door handle replacement, and the new one has to match the screw spacing and the lock it works with. If the old lever was small and stiff, ask whether a handle set that fits your door's holes comes with an easier lever.

## The mirrored doors inside

Many condo units have more than one slider. Bypass closet doors, often mirrored, roll on their own small wheels and jump the track when the top guide sits too low or the bottom rollers wear. Our [closet door page](/sliding-closet-door-repair) explains how to tell a top-hung door from a bottom-rolling one. A mirrored panel is heavy, so lifting one back on takes two people.

The screen panel outside the glass door is another small-parts job. Sliding screen door repair usually comes down to mesh, spline or the little spring rollers that keep it on its rail.

## When the door needs replacing

A new door needs a permit. The city lists windows, doors and shutters among the structural items accepted at walk-through permitting on Tuesday and Thursday mornings, at 10770 W. Oakland Park Boulevard. Call ahead, since walk-in hours can change.

We'll tell you when a sliding glass door replacement makes more sense than another round of parts: a damaged frame, compromised glass, or corrosion across several failing components. On a sound frame, a sliding glass door roller replacement is a much smaller job.

A door stuck half open needs to be moving today, and the first look goes to the rollers and track.

A multi-panel slider or a French door is a different kind of patio door repair. A photo of the whole door and its handle tells us what you're dealing with. A sliding patio door repair on a balcony door follows the same order: track, rollers, then the lock.
