# Brief: sliding-glass-door-repair-boca-raton

Primary keyword: sliding glass door repair Boca Raton. Check date for all sources: 2026-11-26.

## 1. Search intent
A Boca Raton homeowner with a slider or a multi-panel glass door that drags, won't lock or leaks at the sill, wanting someone who can work on it. Secondary: an owner whose door may need replacing, wanting to know what the city and county require now that they're outside the HVHZ.

## 2. SERP note (search of the primary keyword, 2026-11-26)
Thumbtack, Angi, HomeAdvisor and BBB listings, a franchise handyman page, a Google Business Profile landing page, and a few local repair companies with generic service lists and cost averages. Nothing explains that Palm Beach County is outside the HVHZ but in the wind-borne debris region, nothing on Boca Raton's permit paperwork, and nothing specific to multi-slide or pocketing doors. We add all three.

## 3. Unique angle
The hurricane rules change at the county line: Boca Raton is outside the HVHZ but inside the storm rules, and its multi-panel doors fail one panel at a time.

Hypothesis change: "larger custom homes with multi-slide doors" and "strict community appearance review" couldn't be verified as Boca-specific facts. The page treats multi-panel doors as a topic (no claim about how common they are) and describes the Community Appearance Board only in the words of the city's page.
