## 1. Search intent

A Miami resident, most likely in a condo or rental apartment, whose balcony slider drags, leaks or won't lock. Two situations: (a) a repair now, where they need to know what's wrong and what to send; (b) a door that may need replacing, often raised by the association or a building inspection report, where they need to know who decides what (association, city, county, state).

## 2. SERP note

Web search "sliding glass door repair Miami" (2026-11-26): the results are almost all lead-gen directories (HomeAdvisor, Angi, Thumbtack, HomeGuide) and directory listings for small repair companies, with HomeAdvisor cost averages. Nothing in the top results explains condo-specific issues: association hurricane protection specifications, milestone inspections, the Miami-Dade 30-year recertification report's door section, or the HVHZ approval a replacement needs. Nothing addresses balcony sills and weep holes. That's the gap this page fills.

## 3. Unique angle

Most Miami sliders are balcony doors in large condo buildings, so the page explains how salt, wind and the paperwork of condo life (association specs, city permit, HVHZ approval, milestone and 30-year recertification) bear on a sliding door, and what stays a simple repair.
