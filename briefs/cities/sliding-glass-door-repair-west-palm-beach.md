# Brief: sliding-glass-door-repair-west-palm-beach

Primary keyword: sliding glass door repair West Palm Beach. Check date for all sources: 2026-11-26.

## 1. Search intent
A West Palm Beach owner, often of an older house, whose slider drags or won't lock, and who may be unsure whether work on a historic house needs city approval. Secondary: someone weighing a replacement door who needs to know about the certificate of appropriateness and the building permit.

## 2. SERP note (search of the primary keyword, 2026-11-26)
HomeAdvisor and Angi listings dominate, with cost averages ($75-$600 range), plus a couple of local company pages and Angi profiles. None mention historic districts, certificates of appropriateness, the city's permit FAQ or Palm Beach County's position outside the HVHZ. We add a COA review table for door work, the "all sides reviewed" rule, and the permit/wind-load requirement.

## 3. Unique angle
In a city with 18 historic districts, a door repair needs no historic approval, but changing the door's material, style or opening does, and who reviews it depends on which wall it's on.

## 5. Business experience used
- Rollers, tracks and locks as the three most common problems (answer 1).
- Older PGT/CGI and discontinued aluminum doors hard to source parts for (answer 3).
- Site practice: match rollers by measurement; say so before ordering if the part isn't made anymore.
