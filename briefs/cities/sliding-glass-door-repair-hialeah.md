## 5. Owner experience used

- Joseph, answer 3: older PGT, CGI and discontinued aluminum doors can need extra effort to source rollers, handles, locks; the challenge is identifying the exact model.
- Joseph, answer 6: buying rollers on appearance rather than measurements is a common mistake.
- Joseph, answer 2: homes farther from the ocean need less frequent maintenance; salt accelerates corrosion.
- Joseph, answers 1 and 5: rollers/tracks/locks; honesty when a door has outlived its parts.
- Site claims reused from the roller page: plastic breaks down in heat and UV; inland nylon or steel is usually fine; stainless for salt.
