# Brief: sliding-glass-door-repair-deerfield-beach

Primary keyword: sliding glass door repair Deerfield Beach. Check date for all sources: 2026-11-26.

## 1. Search intent
A Deerfield Beach owner (often a condo owner) whose slider is hard to open, won't lock, or has a screen falling off. Some are in beach-area buildings with salt-exposed balcony doors; others are in inland condo communities such as Century Village East. Secondary intent: someone planning a new door who needs to know what Broward County and their condo association require.

## 2. SERP note (search of the primary keyword, 2026-11-26)
Results are lead-gen directories (HomeAdvisor, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB), a few local repair-company pages with generic copy ("doors that are hard to open", "15 years experience"), and a templated city page on a netlify site. None mention Broward's HVHZ status, the county's uniform retrofit form, condo hurricane-protection law, or the difference between beach and inland condo doors. We add: a beach vs. inland comparison, the three rule-makers for a new door (code, association, city), and a pre-call checklist built around the declaration of condominium.

Two kinds of condo slider in one city, beach balcony and inland condo village, with the same parts wearing at different speeds and three sets of rules on any replacement.

## 5. Business experience used
- Rollers, tracks and locks are the three most common failures (Joseph, interview answer 1).
- Coastal homes need more frequent maintenance than inland (answer 2), used to frame the table.
- Older discontinued aluminum doors can be hard to source parts for (answer 3).
- Site practice: check the track before the rollers, match rollers by measurement not looks, panel height trade-off.
