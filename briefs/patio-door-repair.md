## 4. Facts and sources (all checked 2026-11-26)

### Door types (definitions)
1. **Sliding/gliding vs. swinging patio doors.** Verified. JELD-WEN Wood Patio Doors Product Guide (JPG012): "Sliding/Gliding patio doors consist of at least two side-by-side panels, one may be a stationary panel and at least one is an operating panel (options also include multiple panel patio doors with three or more panels). The operating panel(s) slide back and forth horizontally... An insect screen is mounted on the exterior side of the operating panel(s). Swinging patio doors swing out on side hinges and may have single-panel or double-panel configurations." Also: single-panel swinging doors open left or right; two or more panels open in many combinations. https://edge.sitecorecloud.io/jeldwencanada1-jeldwen-p523-e8d9/media/Project/JeldWen/JeldWenCanada/Articles/Documents-Video/Care-Maintenance/JPG012_5.pdf
2. **Sliding glass door (patio-type) legal definition.** Verified. 16 CFR 1201.2(a)(31): "an assembly of one or more panels, at least one of which is suitably movable" for entry/exit; covers fixed and movable panels. https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/1201.2
3. **Multi-slide pocketing doors slide into a wall pocket that needs its own drainage/panning.** Verified. Marvin Modern MultiSlide Pocket installation instructions (2025-12-15): "Pocket panning must have a minimum of 4" (102) end dam on all sides of the pocket." Per-panel roller adjustment: pry the panel up with a block of wood to take weight off the rollers, remove the roller adjustment hole cover, use a 5 mm hex wrench, check for an even reveal against the jamb. https://marvin.com/f/1019562/x/d4370fec37/modern-multislide-pocket-installation-instructions-19915885.pdf
4. **Multi-slide sills come in various riser heights and drain through a weep system; sill pans required.** Verified. Loewen MultiSlide Installation Guide: "MultiSlide sills come in a variety of riser heights"; sill assembly slopes to "allow water run-off from weep system"; "Sill Pans must be used in every installation of a Loewen MultiSlide system." https://www.loewen.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/Loewen-MultiSlide-InstallationGuide.pdf

### Sliding patio doors: common failures and adjustments
5. **Stiff sliding / uneven gap = panel out of alignment; adjustable up or down.** Verified. Andersen help center: https://helpcenter.andersenwindows.com/aw/articles/Knowledge/Adjust-Operating-Panel-on-Andersen-Gliding-Patio-Doors
6. **Roller height trade-off.** Verified. JELD-WEN JPG012: "Adjusting door too high may allow water and air leakage. Adjusting rollers too low can cause panel to drag on lower track." Adjust in 1/4 turns by hand, not with a power driver; direction depends on roller type. (Keep brief here; roller page owns detail.)
7. **Plastic hardware degrades from UV and heat; metal hardware is not totally corrosion proof.** Verified. JELD-WEN JPG012, Hardware Types.

### Hinged (French/swinging) patio doors: common failures and adjustments
8. **Sag / uneven reveal is corrected at the hinges.** Verified. JELD-WEN JPG012, Hinge Adjustment for Swinging Doors: correct alignment with hinge adjustment screws "1/4 turn at a time"; standard non-adjustable hinges are corrected with hinge shims behind the hinge plate; adjustable inswing hinges have horizontal (H) and horizontal-vertical (H-V) types; outswing adjustable hinges have horizontal and vertical adjustment ("To lift the door: Turn vertical adjustment Allen screw of the top hinge clockwise; do not adjust up beyond the top indicator line"). Tilting the door: adjust top hinge opposite to bottom hinge.
9. **Latch misses = strike adjustment; shims behind strike plate.** Verified. JELD-WEN JPG012, Lock Adjustment (swinging): loosen strike screws, move strike up or down to align with latch hooks; shims can be placed behind the strike plate.
10. **French doors often have an active and a passive panel with an astragal, and many use multipoint locks.** Verified. JELD-WEN JPG012 describes multi-point swinging door locks, passive panel with astragal, and lock handing changes. Outswing hinges may have non-removable pins (set screw on barrel). Loewen maintenance doc: "Use compressed air to remove sand and salt from the multipoint lock mechanism" then lubricate with waterproof lubricant. https://www.loewen.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/Maintenance-of-Loewen-Products-1.pdf

### Florida conditions (keep short; homepage owns the full Florida section, avoid overlap)
11. **Coastal maintenance interval.** Verified. Loewen: every 3 months in coastal/harsh environments vs. 6 months typical; remove sand, salt and grit from mechanisms; clean threshold weep holes with a soft brush; water draining from sill weep holes is normal. URL item 10.
12. **Wind-driven rain enters through sliding door sills; sill risers matter; all Florida is hurricane-prone.** Verified. Florida Building Commission/FIU-UF report, Nov 2024: https://www.floridabuilding.org/fbc/publications/Research_2024-2025/Final%20Report%20-%20Reviewing%20the%20Standards%20for%20Wind-Driven%20Rain%20Through%20Tracks%20of%20Sliding%20Glass%20Doors%20systems.pdf (Use at most one line here; homepage uses it more.)
13. **Hurricane season June 1 to Nov 30.** Verified. NOAA CPC: https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/outlooks/hurricane2026/May/hurricane.shtml (Probably homepage-only; avoid repeating.)

### Glass (all types)
14. **Patio doors fall under federal safety glazing rules.** Verified. 16 CFR 1201.1(a) covers "Doors" and "Sliding glass doors (patio-type)"; sliding glass doors are Category II; other doors are Category I unless any single glazing piece exceeds 9 sq ft, then Category II (1201.2(a)(3)-(4)). https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/1201.2 . Relevant for French doors with large glass lites.
15. **Fogged glass on any patio door type = failed IGU seal.** Verified. Pella: https://pressroom.pella.com/what-causes-windows-to-fog/ (one line here; homepage owns explanation).
