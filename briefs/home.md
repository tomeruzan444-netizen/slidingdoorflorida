## 1. Search intent

The searcher has a sliding glass door that is misbehaving right now: it sticks or grinds, jumps off the track, will not latch, lets in water or air, has fog between the panes, or has cracked glass. Most do not yet know which part has failed, so they cannot search for the specific service. They want three things fast: what is probably wrong, whether it is a repair or a replacement, and someone in their part of Florida who can come out. "Near me" variants signal intent to call, not to read a DIY guide. A smaller group is comparing DIY vs. hiring and wants to know what is safe to try first.

## 4. Facts and sources (all checked 2026-11-26)

### Florida conditions
1. **Atlantic hurricane season officially runs June 1 through November 30; peak months are August-September-October.** Verified. NOAA Climate Prediction Center, 2026 Atlantic Hurricane Season Outlook: https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/outlooks/hurricane2026/May/hurricane.shtml (quote: "The North Atlantic hurricane season officially runs from June 1st through November 30th."). nhc.noaa.gov returned 403 to our fetcher; CPC is a NOAA primary source.
2. **Coastal environments speed up wear.** Verified. Loewen "Maintenance of Loewen Products including Coastal and Harsh Environments" states that "the combination of constant moisture, salt, and powerful UV rays create an environment for accelerated degradation of all building products, including metal, wood and painted surfaces." Recommends cleaning/inspection every 6 months in typical environments and **every 3 months in coastal and harsh environments**, including inspecting door thresholds and tracks and removing "sand, salt, and grit from operating mechanisms", using compressed air for sand and salt. Also notes that poorly maintained products in coastal settings are not covered by its warranty. https://www.loewen.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/Maintenance-of-Loewen-Products-1.pdf (manufacturer-specific; present as one manufacturer's guidance, not a universal rule).
3. **Plastic hardware degrades from UV and heat; metal hardware is "not totally corrosion proof."** Verified. JELD-WEN Wood Patio Doors Product Guide (JPG012), Hardware Types section. https://edge.sitecorecloud.io/jeldwencanada1-jeldwen-p523-e8d9/media/Project/JeldWen/JeldWenCanada/Articles/Documents-Video/Care-Maintenance/JPG012_5.pdf
4. **All of Florida is a hurricane-prone region, and wind-driven rain enters through sliding glass doors.** Verified. Florida Building Commission research report "Reviewing the Standards for Wind-Driven Rain Intrusion through Tracks of Sliding Glass Doors Systems During Hurricanes" (FIU/UF, revised Nov 8, 2024, prepared under Section 32 of HB 1021 (2024)). It quotes FEMA's Hurricane Ian MAT report: water intrusion observed "primarily through SGDs and soffits", including homes built in 2021; some SGDs "lack adequate sill risers"; and "The entire State of Florida is within a hurricane-prone region." A TAC member stressed "keeping weep holes open." https://www.floridabuilding.org/fbc/publications/Research_2024-2025/Final%20Report%20-%20Reviewing%20the%20Standards%20for%20Wind-Driven%20Rain%20Through%20Tracks%20of%20Sliding%20Glass%20Doors%20systems.pdf
5. **Some water in the sill track during rain is normal; weep holes let it drain out.** Verified. Loewen maintenance doc: "It is normal for water to accumulate and drain out of weep holes found at door sill and thresholds... this area should be kept free from dirt, insects, leaves and debris"; clean weep holes with a small soft brush. (URL in item 2.) Milgard also publishes a weep-hole cleaning guide (putty knife, pipe cleaner, pour water to test) at https://www.milgard.com/how-to-guides/how-to-clean-weep-holes-on-a-vinyl-sliding-door, but the page returned 403 to our fetcher; content seen only in search snippets. **UNVERIFIED (direct fetch failed)**; use Loewen as the cited source.

### Diagnosis mechanics
6. **Door drags or sticks / uneven gap = panel out of alignment; rollers adjust up or down.** Verified. Andersen: "If the operating panel moves stiffly, it may be dragging slightly because the door is out of alignment"; "If the gap is not equal from the top to the bottom of the frame... the operating patio door panel requires adjustment"; panels "can be adjusted up or down in the unit frame." https://helpcenter.andersenwindows.com/aw/articles/Knowledge/Adjust-Operating-Panel-on-Andersen-Gliding-Patio-Doors
7. **How roller adjustment screws work.** Verified. JELD-WEN JPG012 (URL item 3): adjustment holes are at both lower ends of the operating panel, on the face or on the end (remove small plastic plugs); lift the panel to take weight off the roller and turn in 1/4-turn increments with a hand screwdriver, not a power driver; direction depends on the roller type (face-adjusted with no sign or "+": clockwise lowers; end-adjusted: clockwise raises), so direction is model-specific. Adjust "just high enough to clear sill track and keep the weatherstrip hidden"; **too high may allow water and air leakage, too low makes the panel drag**; check for an even, parallel gap and align interlocks and jambs. Pella 250 Series service instruction: #2 Phillips in the adjustment hole on each side of the vent panel, raise or lower until the panel stile is plumb with the frame jamb. https://assets.contentstack.io/v3/assets/bltf589e66bcaecd79c/blt756b7ace1ebe1e02/60acf50c94bb755c04c28d3c/250_spd_panel_roller.pdf
8. **Won't latch often traces to alignment; strike/keeper is adjustable.** Verified. JELD-WEN JPG012: single-point lock hook length adjusts with a screw on the lock face; the keeper/strike moves up or down to align with the lock. Use only as a general pointer on the homepage; lock detail belongs to the lock page.
9. **Fog between panes = failed insulated glass seal.** Verified. Pella: fog appears when "the seal between the panes of glass fails and allows moisture to leak in"; the desiccant "will eventually become saturated"; "Eventually, fogging may develop between the panes of any insulating glass product." Pella notes most of its warranties cover glass at least 20 years (check the door's own warranty). https://pressroom.pella.com/what-causes-windows-to-fog/ . The fix is replacing the insulated glass unit (or panel); the Pella page does not itself say this. Forum and contractor sources agree fog cannot be cleaned out. **"Glass-only IGU replacement is usually possible" = UNVERIFIED** against a manufacturer source; owner to confirm what they do.
10. **Hose/pressure-washing doors can cause IGU seal failure.** **UNVERIFIED.** A manufacturer care sheet seen during research said this, but its source URL could not be confirmed, and PGT's care guide (which reportedly says to avoid power washers/garden hoses) returned 403. Omit unless a fetchable manufacturer source is found. (Loewen, item 2, recommends a "low to medium pressure water rinse" for painted frames, so manufacturer advice varies.)

### Glass and safety
11. **Sliding glass doors (patio-type) are covered by the federal safety glazing standard.** Verified. 16 CFR 1201.1(a) scope lists "Sliding glass doors (patio-type)"; 1201.2(a)(4)(iii) places them in **Category II** (the higher impact class). https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/1201.1 and https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/1201.2 (eCFR redirected our fetcher; Cornell LII mirrors the official text; link eCFR on the page: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-II/subchapter-B/part-1201).
12. **Tempered glass cannot be cut, drilled or ground after tempering without breaking.** Verified. 16 CFR 1201.2(a)(33). Practical meaning: a cracked tempered pane cannot be trimmed or patched; it is replaced with a pane made to size.
13. **Laminated glass = two or more glass layers bonded to plastic interlayer(s).** Verified. 16 CFR 1201.2(a)(13). Relevant because impact doors use laminated glass, which stays in the frame when cracked. ("Impact doors use laminated glass" is general knowledge; not separately sourced: **UNVERIFIED** here; replacement page covers it.)
