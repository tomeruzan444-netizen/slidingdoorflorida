## 1. Search intent

Three overlapping groups land on these keywords:

1. **Door is hard to slide right now** ("sliding glass door roller replacement", "sliding door roller replacement"). They suspect the wheels, want to know if that is really the problem, whether an adjustment fixes it, and whether to DIY or call someone. Many have a heavy panel scraping the sill, a door that jumps the track, or a grinding sound.
2. **Parts buyers** ("sliding door rollers"). A large share of this query is shopping intent: the SERP is full of Lowe's, Ace, Home Depot, Do it Best and parts-supplier listings. These people want to identify and order the right roller. Give them real value (how to identify and measure, what the numbers on a listing mean, why "universal" rollers often do not fit), then explain honestly when a swap is a two-person job or better handed to a technician.
3. **Coastal Florida owners** whose rollers keep failing. They want to know why (salt, sand, humidity) and which roller material holds up.

The page should answer, in order of urgency: is it the roller or something else, try the adjustment screw first, how to identify the roller you have, what to buy, DIY or hire.

## 4. Facts and sources (check date for all: 2026-11-26)

Legend: **Verified** = fetched and read the primary document. **Snippet** = seen only in search-result text, page not fetched; re-check before publishing. **UNVERIFIED** = no adequate source found.

### Adjustment comes first

1. **Rollers have a height-adjustment screw reached through holes at both lower ends of the panel (on the face or on the end); plastic plugs may cover them.** Verified. JELD-WEN Wood Patio Doors Product Guide (JPG012), "Sliding/Gliding Roller Adjustment and Replacement".
   https://edge.sitecorecloud.io/jeldwencanada1-jeldwen-p523-e8d9/media/Project/JeldWen/JeldWenCanada/Articles/Documents-Video/Care-Maintenance/JPG012_5.pdf
2. **Direction is not universal.** Verified, same JELD-WEN doc: face-adjusted rollers with no sign or a (+) sign: clockwise lowers the panel, counterclockwise raises it; opposite for a (-) sign. End-adjusted rollers (Phillips): clockwise raises, counterclockwise lowers. Page note: tell readers to make a small turn and check, not to trust a single rule.
3. **Adjust correctly, not just "up".** Verified, JELD-WEN JPG012: "Adjusting door too high may allow water and air leakage. Adjusting rollers too low can cause panel to drag on lower track." Adjust "just high enough to clear sill track and keep the weatherstrip hidden"; you may need to raise one roller and lower the other; check for an even, parallel gap. Lift the panel to take weight off the roller, turn in 1/4-turn increments with a hand screwdriver, "do not use a power driver".
4. **Pella example.** Verified. Pella 250 Series Sliding Patio Door Roller Adjustment and Replacement service instruction (applies 07/2019 to current): open panel 6-10 in., #2 Phillips in the adjustment hole on each side, "Raise or lower roller until panel stile is plumb with frame jamb." Replacement: open 6 in., lift panel 1 in. to clear the track, remove panel, place on edge, remove roller attachment screws.
   https://assets.contentstack.io/v3/assets/bltf589e66bcaecd79c/blt756b7ace1ebe1e02/60acf50c94bb755c04c28d3c/250_spd_panel_roller.pdf
5. **Andersen: a stiff panel may be dragging because it is out of alignment; check the gap top to bottom; panels adjust up or down; after adjusting, the latch receiver may need adjusting.** Verified (WebFetch summary of Andersen help center article; article does not state direction).
   https://helpcenter.andersenwindows.com/aw/articles/Knowledge/Adjust-Operating-Panel-on-Andersen-Gliding-Patio-Doors
   Note: the latch point belongs to the lock page; one line max here.

### Identifying and measuring a roller

6. **Most aluminum sliding glass doors use single-assembly rollers, most are steel, some nylon; the best way to know what you need is to remove the old rollers and match them.** Verified. All About Doors & Windows (parts supplier), "How to Change the Rollers on Aluminum Sliding Glass Doors" (published 2/23/2012).
   https://www.allaboutdoors.com/pdf/articles/146aluminumslidingdoorrollers.pdf
7. **Same All About Doors doc:** rollers "adjust via adjustment screw on side of roller body"; set new rollers to the lowest position before installing; on many aluminum doors rollers can be changed without removing the panel from the frame by removing the stiles; "Adjust both sides equally"; then check that the lock lines up with the strike; do not hit aluminum with a hammer head (it dents). Verified.
8. **Rollers are not universal; listings tell buyers to compare the line drawing/dimensions before buying.** Snippet (Lowe's Prime-Line listings: "refer to the line art drawing for dimensions and compare to your needs before purchasing", "Not Universal"; Ace: bring the old roller assembly to the store).
9. **Dimensions retailers list** (use as the infographic's measurement set): wheel diameter, wheel width, wheel edge profile (flat, concave, center groove), housing width, housing height, housing length, adjustment location (rear/end, side, top), mounting style (tabs/ears into slots in the bottom rail, plain back, bottom-mount), single vs. tandem (two wheels in line). Snippet, from multiple listings:
   - CRL D1840: 1 in. steel ball-bearing wheel, 3/4 in. (19 mm) wide, 1-3/16 in. (30 mm) tall adjustable steel housing with tabs.
   - CRL 1-1/2 in. steel ball-bearing roller with center groove, wheel 5/16 in. (8 mm) wide.
   - Prime-Line: 1-1/4 in. dia. x 9/16 in. W x 2-5/16 in. L steel roller, diecast adjustable housing; another 1-1/4 in. x 3/4 in. W x 2-5/8 in. L.
   - Prime-Line tandem: two in-line 1-1/4 in. concave nylon ball-bearing rollers in a stamped steel housing with rear adjustment; another tandem with "bottom-mount housing".
   - Barton Kramer: 1-1/2 in. D x 2.125 in. L stainless steel assembly; 1-11/16 in. D x 3.688 in. L aluminum assembly.
   - Housing examples: Height 1 in., Width 3/4 in., Length 3-1/8 in.; another Height 1-1/32 in., Width 11/16 in.; CRL "23/32 in. wide housing" for Marshall doors.
   - Common wheel diameters seen: 1 in., 1-1/8 in., 1-1/4 in., 1-1/2 in., 1-11/16 in.
   Page use: illustrative ranges only, never "the standard size". Re-check any figure before it is printed.
10. **Replacement wheels alone exist for some assemblies** (a Barton Kramer replacement wheel "replaces the regular steel or nylon wheel"; its precision bearing is "factory greased and completely sealed"). Snippet. Some wheels are riveted to the housing, in which case the whole assembly is replaced (marketplace guide; Snippet, low authority).
11. **Sold one per package; two assemblies per sliding door panel** (Ace listing). Snippet. Tandem doors and very large panels may differ: owner to confirm.
12. **Roller weight rating matters.** Verified. FGIA's AAMA 906 is the "Specification for Sliding Door and Lift and Slide Roller Assemblies", covering roller assemblies with or without height adjustment. Under the 2021 update (AAMA 906-21), "representative samples of door rollers are evaluated for performance at the weight rating specified by the roller manufacturer by cycling the rollers under load for 10,000 cycles"; assemblies "ranging from weight capacities of 75 pounds to more than 600 pounds will be tested to proportionate levels of operating force." Source: Building Design + Construction report quoting FGIA (fetched), FGIA news URL (fetch blocked by bot challenge), FGIA store listing.
   https://www.bdcnetwork.com/home/news/55164094/specification-for-sliding-door-lift-and-slide-roller-assemblies-updated
   https://fgiaonline.org/news/fgia-updates-specification-for-sliding-door-lift-and-slide-roller-assemblies
   https://store.fgiaonline.org/AAMA-906-15
   Page use: a heavy impact panel needs a roller rated for its weight; do not claim any specific product is AAMA 906 compliant unless its maker says so.

### Materials, bearings, salt

13. **Metal hardware "is not totally corrosion proof. Replace corroded hardware." Plastic hardware "can deteriorate from ultraviolet light, heat, cold, and chemical exposure."** Verified. JELD-WEN Care and Maintenance, Aluminum Windows and Patio Doors (JCM001, 12/17), Hardware Types. Same doc: maintain hardware at least twice a year, "monthly for coastal areas".
    https://pdf.lowes.com/productdocuments/19107816-1cdb-4f22-abd2-e68d75a86980/01120390.pdf (JELD-WEN document hosted on Lowe's PDF server; original listed at jeld-wen.com/resources)
14. **Galvanic corrosion mechanism.** Verified. NOAA OMAO "Classic Galvanic Corrosion": dissimilar metals in contact "in the presence of an electrolyte, in this case sea water"; aluminum is the less noble metal and becomes the anode and corrodes; stainless steel is closer to bronze on the galvanic scale.
    https://www.omao.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/documents/ClassicGalvanicCorrosion.pdf
15. **Sea chloride drives atmospheric corrosion; "salt is a very effective and abundant electrolyte"; aluminum's oxide "may be susceptible to attack from strong cleaners or heavy salt deposits"; the Aluminum Association "highly recommends stainless steel fasteners for use with aluminum structures" in coastal settings.** Verified (secondary trade source quoting Aluminum Association). The Construction Specifier, "Selecting aluminum finishes for coastal areas".
    https://www.constructionspecifier.com/selecting-aluminum-finishes-for-coastal-areas/2/
16. **Salt, moisture and UV accelerate degradation of metal; use compressed air to remove sand and salt from mechanisms.** Verified. Loewen, "Maintenance of Loewen Products" (coastal/harsh environments). Homepage brief already uses Loewen's coastal interval; use at most one line here and do not repeat the interval.
    https://www.loewen.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/Maintenance-of-Loewen-Products-1.pdf
17. **Plastic (nylon) wheels described as rust resistant; steel and stainless wheels widely sold with ball bearings, some with sealed bearings.** Snippet (Zoro/Prime-Line and Ace listings).
18. **How salt specifically kills a roller (rusted steel axle or bearing, seized wheel, flat-spotted nylon, corroded steel housing against aluminum rail).** UNVERIFIED as a sourced failure mode. The corrosion mechanism (items 14-15) is sourced; the roller-specific failure pattern must come from the owner's job experience (see section 5).
19. **Delrin (acetal) wheels.** UNVERIFIED. No retailer or manufacturer listing naming Delrin was found in this research. Do not mention Delrin unless the owner uses/stocks it and a product spec confirms it.
20. **Ball-bearing vs. sleeve (plain) bearing comparison.** UNVERIFIED for "sleeve". Ball-bearing rollers are documented in listings; no source found describing sleeve-bearing patio rollers. Mention only if owner confirms they see them.
21. **Roller lifespan in years.** UNVERIFIED. Only competitor sales pages and low-quality vendor blogs give numbers (for example "8 to 12 years in Florida"). Do not publish a number.

### Lubrication (manufacturers disagree)

22. **JELD-WEN (aluminum patio doors): "Lubricate rollers where the shaft goes through the middle of the wheels with light oil."** Verified, JCM001 "Patio Door Rollers and Multi-Point Locks". Same section: "Removing patio door panels can be dangerous and should be performed by service personnel or at least with additional help."
23. **PGT: wheels "do not need lubrication since they contain a sealed ball bearing housing."** UNVERIFIED (seen only in a search snippet of PGT's Care & Maintenance PDF; pgtwindows.com returned 403 to every fetch). Page use: if both are kept, frame it as "check your maker's care sheet", not as a rule.

### DIY vs. hire

24. **Panel removal is a two-person or professional job per manufacturers.** Verified: JELD-WEN JCM001 (item 22); All About Doors (lift with a partner, large suction cup); Pella (lift panel 1 in. to clear track). iFixit and Milgard also say two people (Snippet; Milgard PDF 403).

## 6. Infographic: labeled SVG roller identification and measuring guide

**Type:** measuring guide (different from homepage symptom finder and every other page). Built as inline SVG with real text, inside `figure` with `figcaption`.

**Question it answers:** "Which roller do I have, and what do I write down before I order one?"

**Layout:**
- **Panel A, side view of a single-wheel roller assembly** with dimension lines:
  - `A: Wheel diameter` (measure edge to edge through the center; example label range "common: 1 in. to 1-1/2 in.")
  - `B: Housing length` (end to end of the metal or plastic body)
  - `C: Housing height` (bottom of housing to top, wheel retracted)
  - `D: Adjustment screw` (arrow to screw at end of housing; sublabel "end, side or top: note which")
  - `E: Mounting screw hole / tabs` (arrow to tab "ears" or screw hole)
- **Panel B, end view (looking along the track):**
  - `F: Housing width` (side to side; example "3/4 in.")
  - `G: Wheel width` (example "5/16 in.")
  - `H: Wheel edge profile` with three mini icons labeled `Flat`, `Concave`, `Center groove`
- **Panel C, single vs. tandem silhouettes** labeled `Single wheel` and `Tandem: two wheels in line`.
- **Panel D, material and bearing chips:** `Nylon/plastic wheel`, `Steel wheel`, `Stainless steel wheel`; `Ball bearing (look for bearing races in the hub)`.
- **"Write this down" checklist box:** wheel diameter, wheel width, edge profile, housing L x W x H, adjustment location, mounting type, single or tandem, wheel material, door brand if a label is visible.
- **Tip strip:** "Measure the old roller with a caliper or ruler in 1/16 in. steps. A worn wheel can measure smaller than new: if in doubt, round up and compare the housing first." (Second sentence is practical guidance from a measuring guide, Snippet; owner to confirm before publishing.)

**figcaption takeaway (direction, not final copy):** rollers are matched by housing and wheel size plus mounting style, not by "universal" fit.

## 7. FAQ candidates (roller-only; not shared with track, lock, screen, closet, replacement or home pages)

Source note: WebSearch does not return the Google "People also ask" box. Sources below are related searches, question-phrased result titles, retailer Q&A and forum snippets. No Reddit/Houzz threads surfaced for these queries.

1. **Can I replace sliding glass door rollers myself?** (Home Depot/Lowe's Q&A, Hometalk, iFixit) Note: often yes with a helper; manufacturers warn panel removal is dangerous and should be done by service personnel or with help (JELD-WEN JCM001).
2. **How do I know if my sliding door rollers are bad?** (related searches; This Old House "cracked or no longer rotate smoothly", Snippet) Note: cracked, flat-spotted or seized wheels, rust; if adjustment does not fix dragging, inspect rollers.
3. **Should I try adjusting the rollers before replacing them?** (related searches) Note: yes; roller height is adjustable on most doors (JELD-WEN, Pella, Andersen).
4. **Which way do I turn the screw to raise my sliding door?** (forum and how-to phrasing, Hunker/Lowe's) Note: depends on the roller; JELD-WEN documents opposite directions by roller type and +/- marks; turn 1/4 at a time and check.
5. **Where is the roller adjustment screw on a sliding glass door?** (related searches) Note: holes at the lower ends of the panel, on the face or the end edge, often under plastic plugs (JELD-WEN, Pella).
6. **Are sliding door rollers universal?** (Lowe's/Ace Q&A) Note: no; match housing, wheel and mounting (Prime-Line listings "Not Universal", All About Doors).
7. **How do I measure a sliding door roller for replacement?** (measuring-guide queries) Note: wheel diameter and width, housing L/W/H, adjustment and mounting style, single or tandem.
8. **What is the difference between single and tandem rollers?** (listing terminology) Note: tandem has two wheels in line in one housing; must match what the door was built for.
9. **Are steel or nylon sliding door rollers better?** (search "nylon vs steel") Note: no sourced universal answer; nylon/plastic is rust resistant (listing), steel is metal hardware that can corrode (JELD-WEN); match the original unless the maker offers an upgrade. Owner input.
10. **Are stainless steel rollers worth it near the beach?** (Florida phrasing; coastal guides) Note: stainless resists salt better than plain steel, and sources favor stainless with aluminum in coastal settings (Construction Specifier citing Aluminum Association); no lifespan claim.
11. **Why do my sliding door rollers keep wearing out?** (forum phrasing) Note: salt and moisture accelerate metal corrosion (NOAA, Loewen); sand grinds wheels; panel set too low drags. Owner experience needed for Florida specifics.
12. **Can I replace just the wheel instead of the whole roller?** (listing for replacement wheels) Note: sometimes; some assemblies take a replacement wheel, riveted ones need the full assembly (Snippet).
13. **Do I have to take the door off to replace the rollers?** (related searches) Note: usually the panel is lifted out (Pella, JELD-WEN); some aluminum doors allow a swap by removing the stiles (All About Doors).
14. **Should I oil my sliding door rollers?** (related searches) Note: makers differ: JELD-WEN says light oil on the wheel shaft; PGT reportedly says sealed bearings need none (UNVERIFIED). Follow your door's care sheet.
15. **How many rollers does a sliding glass door have?** (listing "2 required per door") Note: typically two per sliding panel, one at each bottom corner; tandem doors use two-wheel housings. Owner to confirm for large panels.
16. **How much weight can sliding door rollers hold?** (heavy impact door context) Note: depends on the roller's rating; AAMA 906 tests at the maker's rated weight, from 75 to over 600 lb assemblies.
17. **Why does my sliding door grind or squeal when it rolls?** (forum phrasing) Note: dirty or failing bearings, cracked wheel, or debris; if the track itself is damaged, that is the track page (link, do not explain).
18. **How do I find the right roller for my door brand?** (parts-buyer intent) Note: brand label on frame/glass if present; parts suppliers catalog by brand; otherwise measure. Avoid overlap with patio-door page "what kind of patio door do I have".
19. **Should I replace both rollers at the same time?** (Lowe's advice for screen rollers, Snippet) Note: no primary source for glass-door rollers; owner practice TO FILL. Drop if owner has no view.
20. **How long does a roller replacement take?** (owner phone question) Note: TO FILL from owner; no invented time.
